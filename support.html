Frequently Asked Questions

What is ClawPhone? ▼

ClawPhone is a lightweight, privacy-first voice chat app that connects you to your personal AI assistant. Instead of typing, you speak naturally and listen to AI responses. It works with custom AI bots and emphasizes privacy over data collection.

How do I get my connection code? ▼

Your connection code comes from your AI bot or ClawPhone command. If you're using ClawBot (our test bot), run this command:

connect clawphone

Your bot will generate a unique code. Copy that code and paste it into the ClawPhone app to connect.

Is my data private? ▼

Yes, completely. ClawPhone does not:

  • Store your messages
  • Track your usage
  • Collect your name or email
  • Share data with third parties
  • Use analytics or advertising

For detailed information, see our Privacy Policy.

Does it work offline? ▼

Core UI features work offline (you can see past conversations). However, to send new messages or receive voice responses, you need an internet connection. Voice synthesis and message transmission require network access.

Can I use ClawPhone with my own bot? ▼

Yes! ClawPhone works with any bot that supports the connection code protocol. If you've built your own AI bot, you can integrate ClawPhone by implementing the connection code flow. See our GitHub documentation for technical details.

Does ClawPhone work on Apple Watch? ▼

Yes! ClawPhone includes a watchOS companion app. You can send messages and receive responses directly from your Watch. Voice input is optimized for quick interactions (great for quick questions while hands are busy).

What voice options are available? ▼

ClawPhone includes multiple voice personas powered by Fish Audio. Currently available voices include natural male/female voices, and character voices (SpongeBob, Patrick, and more). You can switch voices anytime in Settings.

Is ClawPhone open source? ▼

Yes! ClawPhone is open source and community-driven. You can find the source code and contribute on GitHub. We welcome bug reports, feature requests, and contributions.

Getting Started

Step 1: Get Your Connection Code

Ask your AI bot to generate a ClawPhone connection code. For example, if using ClawBot:

/connect clawphone

Your bot will respond with a unique 6-8 character code.

Step 2: Open ClawPhone

Launch the ClawPhone app on your iPhone.

Step 3: Enter Connection Code

On the main screen, tap "Connect" and paste your connection code. The app will verify the code instantly.

Step 4: Start Talking

Once connected, tap the microphone button and speak naturally. ClawPhone will transcribe your voice and send it to your bot. When your bot responds, you'll hear it spoken aloud.

Step 5: Explore Settings (Optional)

In Settings, you can:

  • Change voice (SpongeBob, Patrick, natural voices, etc.)
  • Adjust speech speed
  • View your privacy status
  • Reconnect to a different bot

Troubleshooting

Issue Solution
Connection code not working • Verify the code is correct (copy-paste, don't type)
• Codes expire after 5 minutes — get a new one
• Check your internet connection
Microphone not working • Check if ClawPhone has microphone permission (Settings > Privacy > Microphone)
• Try closing and reopening the app
• Restart your iPhone if permission was recently granted
No audio response • Check if your device volume is up
• Verify Bluetooth headphones (if connected) have battery
• Check internet connection (voice requires network)
• Try a different voice in Settings
App crashes on startup • Force close the app (swipe up from bottom)
• Restart your iPhone
• If persists, uninstall and reinstall ClawPhone
Voice is cutting out • Check WiFi/cellular signal strength
• Move closer to router or away from interference
• Try another voice (some voices are clearer on weak signals)
Connection keeps dropping • Verify bot server is online
• Check firewall/network settings on bot side
• Try reconnecting with a new connection code
Can't use Watch app • Ensure Watch app installed and up-to-date
• Verify iPhone and Watch are paired
• Both devices need internet connection
• Try restarting Watch
Still need help? Email support@clawphone.app with details about your issue.
